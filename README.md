
# Superstore Sales Dashboard 📊

## Business Problem
Identify sales trends, profit leakages, and top-performing regions/categories for a US-based Superstore using 4 years of data.

## Tools Used
**Power BI Desktop** | **DAX** | **Power Query** | **Excel**

## Project Duration
**Start Date:** 15 Sep 2026  
**End Date:** 22 Sep 2026

## Key Features & KPIs
- **Total Sales:** $2.3M | **Total Profit:** $286K | **Profit Margin:** 12.4%
- Interactive filters for Region, Category, Sub-Category, Ship Mode
- YoY Sales Growth trend with DAX time intelligence
- Top 5 Customers & Loss-making Products table

## Key Insights
1. **West Region** drives 32% of total sales but Central region runs in -3% loss
2. **Technology category** has highest profit margin at 17.2% 
3. **Tables & Bookcases** are top loss-making sub-categories
4. **Standard Class** shipping causes 60% of delayed deliveries
